# Dashboard quick controls

The home page now provides private-conversation reply and companion proactive-message switches, together with QQ login status and an administrator-only QR sign-in dialog. Existing controls under Custom rules remain available; their conversation-service settings are reused by the home card.

NapCat login credentials are read by the server from the configured read-only file. Online status reads preserve the current QQ session, and QR refresh is an explicit action while logged out.
